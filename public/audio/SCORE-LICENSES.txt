ORIGINAL SCORE
The eight compositions, nest-lantern leitmotif, orchestration, performance
events, sample-rendering code, and resulting music-* masters were created
specifically for BugWar in this production. No existing song, AI music
service output, General MIDI soundtrack, or oscillator-generated instrument
was used. The original composition/arrangement and rendered music are also
dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal by this production, to the extent the
